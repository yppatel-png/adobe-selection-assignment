## 3. Provenance & Verification Protocol

1. **Tier 1 (Official Verified)**: Extracted directly from official institute documents (`courses of study.pdf`). Page citations are documented in `source_page`.
2. **Tier 2 (Derived Academic Mapping)**: Bipartite links between courses and granular skills (`course_skills.json`) derived directly from published syllabus topic clauses.
3. **Tier 3 (Explicit Operational Assumptions)**: When institute guidelines indicate a range or leave a procedural detail to faculty discretion (such as standard minimum credit thresholds or weekly self-study ratios), these are labeled `provenance="assumption"` and surfaced transparently in the user interface.
